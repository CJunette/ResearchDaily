# AI 新动向

Find early AI ideas, surprising applications and useful conceptual challenges before conference publication. This fifth section is distinct from both adjacent-field established research and non-AI serendipity.

- Read `config/sources.json.ai_frontier`. Use recent category listings/RSS for cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.CV, cs.HC, cs.RO and cs.MA. Sort by newest available submission/update in the configured rolling window (currently 30 days); the run date is not a publication-date requirement. If no strong item is found there, widen to the configured fallback window (currently 90 days), label the age, and choose the newest eligible item. Do not scan every feed in full daily; use saved checkpoints and bounded screening batches. Cross-listings and replacements are not new papers; keep canonical arXiv IDs without version for deduplication and record the actual version read separately.
- arXiv provides category updates; tools featured by arXivLabs (for example CORE recommender) are third-party discovery aids, not arXiv editorial endorsements or peer review. Recommendation lists can supply leads, never substitute for reading or supply unverified popularity claims. Check availability at use time; do not create accounts or upload private preferences to third parties without authorization.
- Favor a specific new problem framing, a meaningful new application/workflow, a surprising result or a testable counterargument. A renamed agent wrapper, a tiny benchmark gain or promotional claims alone are insufficient. Deliberately avoid ranking solely by likes, company name or author fame.
- Use track `ai_frontier` only for genuinely unreviewed preprints/technical reports. Verify publication status: if already accepted/published, route to the appropriate established-research section. This section may discuss concept papers, but distinguish argument from demonstrated result and state what would test it. Quality threshold remains unchanged.
- Read the full manuscript before recommendation, including evidence and limitations. Record `peer_review_status: unreviewed` and `version: <actual version/date>` on separate lines in `reviewer_notes`, as well as `topic:`. The importer validates the first two fields for this track. Explain the idea, exact delta, evidence available, unresolved questions, and potential research use in the usual review fields. Do not present proposed benefits as measured findings.
- Output 0–1 items, typically 3–5 reader minutes, within the same 35-minute ceiling. First reserve the increased original-section minimums, then consider this item before other extras. If no strong candidate or time remains, leave the section visible with an honest empty note and say whether the 30-day and 90-day windows were exhausted. This does not count as an original-section quota shortage. Do not repeat an item in another section or future edition just because its version/venue changes.

Official discovery documentation: https://info.arxiv.org/help/rss.html and https://info.arxiv.org/labs/showcase.html . Save the exact feed/recommendation provenance and retrieval time in the daily run log.
